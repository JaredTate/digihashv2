DigiHash Pool Stats

What do these words mean?
Luck
About how long the pool (or your miner) needs, on average, to find one block at the hashrate it has now. Shorter is better.
Share %
The pool's part of the whole network hashrate for this algorithm.
Round shares
Work sent since the pool last found a block on this algorithm. Your part of the next block reward follows your part of these.
Efficiency
Valid shares as a percent of all shares a worker sent. Low efficiency means many rejected shares.
Valid / Invalid shares
Shares the pool accepted or rejected (for example stale or too easy).
Orphaned
A block the network did not keep. It pays no reward. Pending blocks wait for 100 confirmations, then they are confirmed and paid.
Difficulty
How hard it is to find a block on the network now. It goes up when more miners join.

All pools in one table.
